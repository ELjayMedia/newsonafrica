## 2. How to pitch a story

### Pitch template

```
Working headline: ____________________
Angle / hook: _______________________
Why now: ___________________________
Key sources: ________________________
Estimated length: ___________________
Deadline: __________________________
Multimedia needs: ___________________
```

### Evaluation criteria

Pitches are assessed on:
- **Newsworthiness** — Is this timely and important?
- **Audience relevance** — Will our readers care?
- **Feasibility** — Can we deliver this with available resources?
- **Originality** — Is this a fresh angle or exclusive?

---

## 3. Article submission workflow

1. **Pitch** — Submit pitch via WordPress or to your assignment editor.
2. **Approval** — Editor reviews and approves/revises.
3. **Draft** — Write the article in WordPress (Draft status).
4. **Editorial review** — Editor reviews for accuracy, tone, structure.
5. **Fact-check** — Key claims verified against sources.
6. **Final approval** — Editor-in-chief or designate signs off.
7. **Publication** — Scheduled or published immediately.
8. **Post-publication** — Corrections logged if needed (see correction notice procedure).

## 5. Style cheat sheet

- **Tone:** Clear, direct, professional. No sensationalism.
- **Lead:** Most newsworthy element first.
- **Sourcing:** Named sources preferred. Anonymous requires editor approval.
- **Attribution:** Attribute all claims. Cross-verify key facts with two sources.
- **Headlines:** Accurate, SEO-friendly, no clickbait.
- **Corrections:** Published promptly with dated note.
- **Sponsored content:** Clearly labeled "Sponsored" or "Paid Partnership."
- **Images:** Properly licensed, credited, AI-generated labeled.

## 6. FAQ

**Q: How do I submit a correction?**
A: Contact your editor with the details. Corrections are logged via WordPress custom fields and display automatically on the article.

**Q: Can I republish my work elsewhere?**
A: Check with your assignment editor. NOA retains editorial rights but we're open to syndication discussions.

**Q: What if I encounter a safety concern during an assignment?**
A: Report it to your editor immediately. NOA prioritizes contributor safety.

**Q: How do I get my press ID?**
A: Apply through the NOA credential management system. IDs are valid for one year.

**Q: Who do I contact for editorial questions?**
A: Reach out to your assignment editor or the Editorial Operations manager.
