# News On Africa — Vision & Mission

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective:** 2026-09-03
**Owner:** Founding Team

---

## Vision

A future where African stories are told with depth, accuracy, and authenticity — by Africans, for the world. News On Africa will become the authoritative source for understanding what is happening across the continent, starting with our home in Eswatini and expanding across the region.

## Mission

News On Africa delivers independent, evidence-based journalism that informs decision-makers, amplifies African perspectives, and holds power to account. We serve corporate marketing and communications teams, engaged citizens, and the broader African diaspora with intelligence they cannot find elsewhere.

## Editorial values

1. **Authenticity** — African voices and perspectives, fairly represented.
2. **Accuracy** — Every claim verified, every error corrected promptly.
3. **Independence** — Editorial integrity free from commercial or political influence.
4. **Solutions journalism** — Highlighting responses to problems, not just the problems themselves.
5. **Pan-African perspective** — Local stories connected to continental and global context.

## Strategic positioning

- **Beachhead customer:** Corporate marketing and communications teams in Eswatini.
- **Primary daily job:** Answer "What changed since yesterday that I need to know?"
- **Geographic focus:** Eswatini edition first, with expansion to Southern Africa and the broader continent.
- **Content pillars:** Politics & governance, business & economy, technology, culture & sports, health & science, and the environment.

## Success metrics

- Subscriber growth and engagement.
- Brand recognition among target corporate customers.
- Editorial quality and accuracy (corrections rate).
- Revenue sustainability through subscriptions and sponsorship.
