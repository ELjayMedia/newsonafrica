# News On Africa — Event Media Accreditation SOP

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective:** 2026-09-03
**Owner:** Editorial Operations

---

## 1. Purpose

This SOP establishes a repeatable process for requesting, approving, and managing media accreditation for government, corporate, sporting, cultural, and international events in Eswatini.

---

## 2. Scope

This applies to all NOA editorial staff, freelancers, photographers, videographers, and contributors who need media access to events on behalf of News On Africa.

---

## 3. Definitions

- **Standing media recognition:** NOA's ongoing accreditation with government and major institutions.
- **Event-specific access:** Temporary accreditation for a single event or assignment.
- **Credential:** NOA press ID card issued to authorized personnel.

---

## 4. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| **Editor-in-Chief** | Final approval on all accreditation requests |
| **Assignment Editor** | Reviews requests, coordinates logistics |
| **Credentials Manager** | Maintains credential register, issues/revokes IDs |
| **Field Crew** | Submits requests, follows event protocols |

---

## 5. Accreditation request process

### 5.1 Initiation
1. Field crew member or editor identifies an event requiring accreditation.
2. Complete the Event Accreditation Request Form (see Appendix A).
3. Submit to Assignment Editor at least **14 days** before the event (or as soon as the event is announced).

### 5.2 Review
1. Assignment Editor verifies the event's newsworthiness and alignment with NOA editorial priorities.
2. Confirm required personnel and equipment.
3. Check for any security, safety, or access concerns.

### 5.3 Approval
1. Assignment Editor recommends approval/denial.
2. Editor-in-Chief gives final sign-off.
3. Credentials Manager is notified of approved personnel.

### 5.4 Credential collection
1. Approved personnel collect their NOA press ID from the Credentials Manager.
2. Verify ID details (name, photo, role, expiry) are correct.
3. Sign the credential register acknowledging receipt.

---

## 6. Accreditation checklist

Before attending an event, confirm the following:

- [ ] Press ID card (valid, not expired)
- [ ] Assignment letter from NOA Editor-in-Chief
- [ ] Passport or national ID (for verification)
- [ ] Press ID lanyard or holder
- [ ] Camera/video equipment (if applicable)
- [ ] Media insurance documents (if required by event)
- [ ] Venue-specific access pass (if pre-arranged)
- [ ] Emergency contact numbers
- [ ] Event-specific rules and restrictions reviewed

---

## 7. Event protocols

- Arrive at least 30 minutes before the event start.
- Present credentials at every security checkpoint.
- Follow all venue rules regarding photography, recording, and movement.
- Do not enter restricted areas without explicit permission.
- Report any access issues or incidents to the Assignment Editor immediately.
- Return equipment and credentials after the event.

---

## 8. Post-event close-out

1. Return all temporary access passes to the Credentials Manager.
2. Complete the Post-Event Report (see Appendix B) within 48 hours.
3. Note any access issues, security incidents, or lessons learned.
4. Update the central contact register with useful contacts made during the event.
5. File any media coverage produced.

---

## 9. Credential management

### Issuance
- New credentials are issued to approved editorial personnel.
- Each credential has a unique number (format: NOA-YYYY-NNNN).
- Credentials are valid for one year from the issue date.

### Renewal
- Credentials expire annually. Renewal requests must be submitted 30 days before expiry.
- Renewal requires confirmation of continued editorial role.

### Suspension and revocation
- Credentials may be suspended or revoked for:
  - Breach of event protocols
  - Misrepresentation of NOA
  - Safety or security violations
  - End of employment or engagement
- Revoked credentials must be returned within 7 days.

### Lost or stolen
- Report lost or stolen credentials to the Credentials Manager immediately.
- A replacement will be issued with a new credential number.
- The old credential number is marked as revoked in the register.
