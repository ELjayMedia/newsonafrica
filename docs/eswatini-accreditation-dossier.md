## 3. Editorial mission and mandate

News On Africa delivers independent, evidence-based journalism that informs decision-makers, amplifies African perspectives, and holds power to account.

**Content pillars:**
- Politics & governance
- Business & economy
- Technology
- Culture & sports
- Health & science
- Environment

**Geographic focus:** Eswatini edition first, with expansion to Southern Africa and the broader continent.

**Primary audience:** Corporate marketing and communications teams, engaged citizens, African diaspora.
